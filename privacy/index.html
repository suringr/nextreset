Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 6, 2026

NextReset ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have when you visit nextreset.co ("the Site").

Information We Collect

NextReset is a static website that does not require user accounts, logins, or personal information to use. We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information directly.

However, we use third-party services that may automatically collect certain technical information:

  • Google Analytics — We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use the Site. This collects anonymous data such as page views, session duration, approximate geographic location (country/city level), device type, browser type, and referring websites. Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish unique visitors. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

The Site itself does not set any first-party cookies. Third-party services we use (Google Analytics) may set cookies to function properly. These cookies are used solely for analytics purposes and do not track you across other websites.

Data We Don't Collect

To be clear about what we do not do:

  • We do not require or store user accounts
  • We do not collect email addresses or personal contact information
  • We do not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • We do not use targeted advertising or behavioral tracking
  • We do not collect any gaming account information or game credentials

Third-Party Services

The Site uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics — For anonymous traffic analytics. Google's Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages — For hosting the Site. GitHub's Privacy Statement

Data Retention

We do not store any user data on our servers. Google Analytics data is retained according to Google's default retention settings (14 months) and is used only in aggregate to understand traffic patterns.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

External Links

The Site contains links to official game publisher websites and other external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

Changes to This Policy
