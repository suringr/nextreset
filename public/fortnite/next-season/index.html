About this tracker

This page tracks the end date of the current Fortnite Battle Pass season, read from the official Battle Pass page on fortnite.com. On 5 April 2026 that source began refusing requests from our servers. While it refuses, this page shows no date rather than an estimate.

Why this page may show no date

On 5 April 2026 the Battle Pass page began returning an HTTP 403 challenge to our requests instead of a page. A check of Epic's other official properties found the same thing: fortnite.com, its news section, the Epic Games news site and the Fortnite store page all answered our servers that way, and Epic's robots.txt was itself behind the challenge, so we could not even read the rules it would like crawlers to follow.

Two things we will not do to fill the gap:

  • Work around the challenge. It is a clear signal about automated access, and NextReset treats it as one.
  • Read the date from somewhere unofficial. Community trackers and third-party Fortnite APIs do publish season dates, and they are often right. They are not the publisher, and this site's one rule is that a date comes from whoever decides it.

The one Epic endpoint that did answer was the service status API, which reports whether Fortnite is up — not when the season ends. It cannot answer this page's question whatever it returns.

Whenever the source does answer, the date it states appears at the top of this page like any other tracker here, with the time it was verified. When it does not, the top of the page says so plainly. Either way the sections below cover what a season change actually does.

What Is the Season End?

Every few months, Epic Games launches a new Fortnite season with a completely refreshed Battle Pass, new map changes, fresh weapons, and unique gameplay mechanics. Each season typically lasts 10-13 weeks and introduces a new theme that reshapes the entire game experience. Knowing when the current season ends helps you plan your remaining Battle Pass challenges and prepare for whatever comes next.

What Changes

  • New Battle Pass with 100+ tiers of cosmetics
  • Map changes — new POIs, terrain updates, and biome shifts
  • Fresh weapon and item loot pool
  • New gameplay mechanics and vehicles
  • Seasonal quests and XP challenges reset
  • Competitive ranked season may reset

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does this page sometimes show no season end date?

Because the only sources we accept are Epic's own, and on 5 April 2026 they began answering our servers with an HTTP 403 challenge rather than a page. We do not work around that challenge, and we do not take season dates from community trackers or unofficial API mirrors, accurate though those often are. When Epic's page answers, the date appears here; when it does not, we would rather show nothing than a guess.

What happens when a Fortnite season ends?

When a season ends, your Battle Pass progress resets, the map typically changes, and a new set of weapons and items are introduced. Any unearned Battle Pass rewards from the previous season become unavailable.

Where can I see the official season end date?

Epic shows the current season's end date on the Battle Pass screen inside Fortnite itself, and announces season changes on fortnite.com. Those are the authoritative places. We would rather point you to them than restate a date we cannot verify.
