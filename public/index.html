What is NextReset?

NextReset is a free, automated tracker that monitors official game publisher sources to show you exactly when your favorite games reset, update, or patch. Unlike community predictions or Reddit rumors, every date on NextReset comes directly from official sources — patch schedules, publisher APIs, and official announcements.

We currently track 12 of the most popular games including Fortnite, League of Legends, VALORANT, Counter-Strike 2, Minecraft, Roblox, GTA Online, Warzone, Genshin Impact, PUBG, Red Dead Redemption 2, and EA SPORTS FC. Each game has a dedicated page with a live countdown, source attribution, and confidence rating.

How It Works

🔍
Monitor

Our system checks official game publisher pages several times a day — patch schedules, news feeds, and status APIs.

✅
Verify

Extracted dates are validated against known patterns and sanity-checked to prevent false data from reaching you.

⏱️
Display

Live countdowns update in real-time on your screen. If a source becomes unavailable, cached data is shown instead.

Why Trust NextReset?

Every piece of data on NextReset comes from official sources. We never guess, predict, or speculate about upcoming dates. If a game publisher hasn't announced a date, we show "Data Unavailable" instead of making something up. Each tracker shows its source URL, confidence level, and when it was last checked so you can verify the data yourself.

Our system is designed to fail gracefully. If a publisher's website goes down or blocks our checks, we preserve the last known good data instead of showing errors. You'll see a "Stale" badge when this happens, so you always know the freshness of what you're looking at.
