Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

NextReset ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have when you visit nextreset.co ("the Site").

Information We Collect

NextReset is a static website that does not require user accounts, logins, or personal information to use. We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information directly.

However, we use third-party services that may automatically collect certain technical information:

  • Google Analytics — We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use the Site. It collects page views, session duration, approximate geographic location (country or city level), device type, browser type, and referring websites, and it uses cookies and similar identifiers to distinguish visitors. Google processes this data on our behalf and we see it only in aggregate. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

The Site itself does not set first-party cookies. Third-party services set the cookies described in this policy: Google Analytics for traffic measurement, and Google advertising cookies set through the AdSense code described under Advertising below. Advertising cookies may be associated with Google domains such as doubleclick.net or country-specific Google domains.

Advertising

NextReset includes the Google AdSense site code on its content pages. That code loads Google's advertising library from Google's servers when you open a page.

The Site contains no ad placements of its own. Whether an advertisement is shown, and where it appears, is decided by Google AdSense account settings and by whether Google has approved the Site to show ads, so there may be no advertising on a given page.

Google and other third-party vendors may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers, to serve and measure ads. These technologies may be used to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can review how Google uses information from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and you can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.

Google requires publishers to use a Google-certified consent management platform before serving personalised ads to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. No consent management platform is configured on the Site at present. We will update this section when that changes.

Data We Don't Collect

To be clear about what we do not do:

  • We do not require or store user accounts
  • We do not collect email addresses or personal contact information
  • We do not sell or trade your personal information
  • We do not collect any gaming account information or game credentials

Third-Party Services

The Site uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics — For traffic analytics. Google's Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — For advertising (see Advertising above). How Google uses data from sites that use its services
  • Cloudflare Pages — For hosting and delivering the Site. Cloudflare's Privacy Policy
  • GitHub — Hosts the open-source repository and the published files. GitHub's Privacy Statement

Data Retention

We do not store any user data on our servers. Google Analytics data is retained according to Google's default retention settings (14 months) and is used only in aggregate to understand traffic patterns.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

External Links

The Site contains links to official game publisher websites and other external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
