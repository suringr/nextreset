How to play ↓

What ONE SHOT is

A sniper arcade game that lives on this site. You watch a street crowd through a scope, and each contract gives you one job: find the right person and take the shot before the clock runs out. There are eighty of them. It is free, it runs in the browser, and there is no account to make and nothing to install.

It was built for this site rather than licensed into it, which is also why it carries no advertising of any kind — not a banner, not a unit, not even the loader script. A game is a bad place to put something a player might hit by accident.

Controls

  • Aim — move the mouse, or drag with a finger on a touchscreen.
  • Shoot — click, press Space, or use the round FIRE button.
  • Reload — press R. From contract 11 you carry more than one round, and running dry halfway through is the usual way to lose one.

What the eighty contracts ask

They are not eighty variations of the same thing. What you are hunting changes, and so does how much room you have to find it:

  • 1–10 — find the courier carrying the red case. One round only: no reload, no second chance.
  • 11–30 — track the moving target, and reload when you run empty. Three rounds.
  • 31–40 — find the armed hostile before he fires at you. Three rounds.
  • 41–60 — precision under pressure. Four rounds.
  • 61–70 — elite contracts, with faster targets.
  • 71–80 — master contracts: survive, then clear the threat.

Three things tighten as you go, which is what makes the late contracts hard rather than merely longer. The time limit falls from twenty seconds to fourteen. The crowd grows from five people to eleven, so there is more to look through. And from contract 31 the hostile shoots back, sooner and sooner: early on he takes roughly seven to nine seconds, and by the last contracts between three and six. He does not take the same time twice, so late contracts are a question of being ready rather than of counting.

Stars, XP and ranks

Each contract is worth up to three stars, and the game keeps the best you have managed on each one, so going back to a contract you scraped through is worth doing. XP accumulates across everything you play, and a rank arrives every 8,000 XP:

Recruit → Rookie → Marksman → Sharpshooter → Hunter → Operative → Elite → Ghost → Master Sniper.

For scale: clearing contract 80 on a streak is worth roughly 100,000 XP, and a perfect run of all eighty about four million.

Where your progress is kept

On your device, in this browser, and nowhere else. There is no account and no save to the cloud, so the game picks up at the highest contract you have unlocked whenever you come back to it in the same browser.

The other side of that is worth knowing before you sink an evening into it: clearing this site's data, playing in a private window, or opening the game on another device starts you again at contract one. Nothing is lost from anywhere else, because it was never anywhere else.
